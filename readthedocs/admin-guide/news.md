# News

The **News** tab edits `streamhub-news.json`, the announcements of the home page and the News page.

```{figure} /_static/screenshots/admin-news.png
:alt: The News tab with four announcements
:class: screenshot

The News tab.
```

- **Add news** adds an announcement on top.
- ✎ edits one: **Title**, **Date** (free text), **Message** and **Type**: *Normal*, *Info* (blue) or *Warning* (orange).
- The arrows reorder them; 🗑 deletes one.

The home page shows the first three. **News** in the top menu lists all of them, and appears only when there is at least one. Entries without a title are skipped. The [News file](../configuration/news.md) page has the JSON format.
